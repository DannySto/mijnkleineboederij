Support for Mijn Kleine Boerderij

What age is the app suitable for?

Mijn Kleine Boerderij is intended for children aged 3 to 5.

How do I check for updates?

Open the parent area and tap Controleer op updates (Check for updates). If an update is available, you can open the App Store.

Can my child play offline?

Yes, all games work offline. You need an internet connection to check for updates.
