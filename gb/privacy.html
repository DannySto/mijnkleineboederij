Privacy Policy for Mijn Kleine Boerderij

  • Effective date 20 November 2026
  • Publisher Danny Stompe
  • Privacy contact Danny Stompe

Children's play and privacy

Mijn Kleine Boerderij is a game for children aged 3 to 5. All games work offline. The app does not ask for an account, name, email address or location. The publisher does not receive personal information or gameplay data from the app. The app contains no ads, tracking or usage analytics. Voice prompts and sounds are built into the app. The app does not request microphone access or record audio.

Settings and game progress

The app stores language and sound preferences, the last selected activity, progress in the tractor and fence games, and the total scores for Egg Hunt and Catch the Apples on the device. This keeps those settings and progress available when you play again. Other game progress is held in the app's memory only while you play.

To erase local data, select Delete App on your iPhone or iPad. App data may be included in a device backup and may return if you restore that backup.

Checking for updates with Apple

When a parent opens the parent area or taps Controleer op updates (Check for updates), the app requests public version information from Apple. The request includes the app's identifier and an App Store country code (the Netherlands or Belgium). Apple may receive technical information through this connection, such as the IP address. The app does not send sound settings, scores or game progress. If an update is available, the parent can open the App Store. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to Apple's processing.

Contact and privacy rights

Parents and guardians can contact the privacy contact listed above with questions about this policy. If you send an email, the publisher receives your email address and the information in your message. The publisher uses this information to respond to your enquiry and keeps it for as long as needed for that purpose, or longer if required by law. Please avoid including information about your child.

For personal information processed by the publisher, you can use the same email address to request access, correction or deletion, among other rights. You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.
